# PlayRFL Copyright and DMCA Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026  
Version: 1.0-draft

RFL respects intellectual-property rights.

## 1. Current Scope

Ordinary users currently do not upload public artwork to PlayRFL.com. Authorized administrators upload Card artwork; users choose display names and communicate through Discord tickets. This Policy must be reviewed if uploads expand.

## 2. Copyright Questions and Notices

A copyright complaint should include:

1. the copyright owner’s or authorized agent’s physical or electronic signature;
2. identification of the copyrighted work, or a representative list;
3. identification and location of the allegedly infringing material, such as the exact URL, Card name/number, and screenshot;
4. the complainant’s name, address, telephone number, and email;
5. a good-faith statement that the use is not authorized by the owner, its agent, or law; and
6. a statement under penalty of perjury that the notice is accurate and the complainant is authorized to act.

Send copyright questions and notices to **PlayRflHelp@gmail.com**.

RFL currently has no formally designated DMCA agent, published agent telephone number or mailing address, and no completed agent registration in the U.S. Copyright Office directory. This email therefore serves only as RFL’s present copyright contact; it must not be described as a registered DMCA agent.

RFL may send the notice to the person responsible for the material and may remove or disable access while reviewing it.

## 3. Counter-Notices

If material was removed by mistake or misidentification, a counter-notice should include:

1. your physical or electronic signature;
2. identification of removed material and its former location;
3. a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief removal resulted from mistake or misidentification;
4. your name, address, and telephone number; and
5. consent to the jurisdiction of the appropriate United States federal district court and acceptance of process from the original complainant or its agent.

RFL may restore material as permitted by 17 U.S.C. § 512 unless the claimant timely reports filing a court action.

## 4. Repeat Infringement and Misrepresentation

RFL may terminate repeat infringers in appropriate circumstances. Knowingly making a material misrepresentation in a notice or counter-notice can create liability.

## 5. Other Intellectual-Property Complaints

Trademark, publicity-right, or other complaints should identify the right, registration if any, exact material, legal basis, and requested action, and be sent to **PlayRflHelp@gmail.com**.

## 6. Required Registration

An email inbox alone is not enough to secure DMCA safe-harbor treatment. RFL must identify its legal service provider, publish the required provider and agent contact information, designate and maintain the agent through the U.S. Copyright Office’s online directory, and keep both records consistent. The Copyright Office requires a service provider physical street address absent an approved safety waiver. A designated agent may be identified by a role or title, and the agent’s mailing address may be a P.O. box. See [the Copyright Office DMCA directory FAQ](https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/faq.html).
