# Realm Fighting League Community Guidelines

Effective date: July 29, 2026  
Version: 1.0-draft

These Guidelines apply on PlayRFL.com, in official Matches and Events, and in official RFL Discord spaces.

## 1. Compete Honestly

- Use permitted clients, controls, and equipment.
- Do not cheat, use unauthorized modifications, macros, scripts, automation, or bots.
- Do not exploit bugs, duplicate Crowns or Cards, bypass limits, or hide an exploit.
- Do not fix Matches, intentionally lose, trade wins, collude on bets, or use nonpublic results for wagering advantage.
- Follow officials’ instructions and Event rules.

Stop using a vulnerability or economy exploit and report it privately.

## 2. Respect People

- No harassment, threats, stalking, doxxing, hate speech, sexual harassment, or targeted humiliation.
- No discrimination based on protected characteristics.
- No sexual content involving minors, grooming, or unsafe attempts to move interactions private.
- Competitive banter must not become abuse.

## 3. Be Authentic

- Do not impersonate RFL, staff, officials, fighters, sponsors, partners, or users.
- Do not falsely claim a result, role, rank, Card, partnership, or endorsement.
- Do not evade moderation through alternate accounts.

## 4. Keep the Community Safe

- No malware, phishing, credential theft, scams, illegal goods, or attack instructions.
- Do not publish private information.
- Do not solicit real-money bets, sell Crowns or Cards for cash, sell accounts, or arrange off-platform trades.
- Do not spam, abuse tickets, or disrupt voice and stream channels.

## 5. Names and Content

Display names and submissions must not be deceptive, obscene, infringing, or filter-evasive. Share only material you have the right to share.

## 6. Enforcement

RFL may consider context, severity, history, intent, cooperation, and harm. Actions include name changes, warnings, Match or bet voids, Crown or Card reversals, role removal, Fighter suspension/removal, Discord moderation, account suspension, and termination.

Serious safety threats, cheating, exploitation, or Match fixing may receive immediate action.

## 7. Reports and Appeals

Use the official Discord help ticket or **PlayRflHelp@gmail.com**. Include names, timestamps, Match IDs, screenshots, and an explanation. Do not retaliate.

There is no on-site report or appeal form; reviews are manual.
