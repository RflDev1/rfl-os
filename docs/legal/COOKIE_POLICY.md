# PlayRFL Cookie Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026  
Version: 1.0-draft

This Policy supplements the Privacy Policy.

## 1. Current Cookies

RFL currently uses only cookies needed for authentication, session continuity, and OAuth security through Auth.js:

| Category | Purpose | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Session | Connects the browser to a database-backed session | Necessary |
| OAuth state, PKCE, and nonce | Protects Discord sign-in against forgery and interception | Necessary; generally short-lived |
| Callback/redirect | Returns you to the intended RFL page | Necessary; generally short-lived |

Exact names and expiry depend on the Auth.js environment and cookie type. A database session can remain until its recorded expiry, sign-out, or invalidation.

## 2. Cookies Not Currently Used

The inspected application has no advertising cookies, cross-site behavioral tracking, third-party analytics tags, marketing pixels, or cookie preference center.

No optional-cookie banner is currently needed for the identified cookies. Before adding nonessential analytics, advertising, or cross-site tracking, RFL must update this Policy and implement consent controls where required.

## 3. Your Controls

You may block or delete cookies through your browser. Doing so may sign you out, interrupt Discord authorization, or prevent authenticated features from working.

Discord and other external sites may use their own cookies under their policies.
