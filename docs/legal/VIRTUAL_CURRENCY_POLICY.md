# PlayRFL Virtual Currency Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026  
Version: 1.0-draft

## 1. Nature of Crowns

Crowns are gameplay-only virtual units recorded in RFL’s database and a limited, revocable permission to use Service features.

Crowns are not money, cryptocurrency, stored value, securities, a bank balance, or personal property. They have no cash value, cannot be bought for money under the current Service, cannot be redeemed or refunded for cash, and cannot transfer outside supported Service transactions.

## 2. Records

RFL maintains a wallet balance and transaction ledger. Current entries include daily rewards, administrative adjustments, casino wagers and payouts, Match bets and payouts, pack purchases, Marketplace purchases and seller proceeds, and reversals.

Database transactions, idempotency keys, and locking reduce but cannot eliminate errors.

## 3. Earning and Use

Eligible users may receive configured rewards. Crowns may be used for available games, bets, packs, and Marketplace transactions, subject to limits and account status.

RFL may prospectively change rewards, wager limits, pack prices, Marketplace limits, and payouts.

## 4. No Off-Platform Market

Do not buy, sell, broker, lend, gift, or exchange Crowns for money, cryptocurrency, goods, services, accounts, or off-platform consideration. RFL will not enforce private transactions.

## 5. Corrections and Forfeiture

RFL may correct, withhold, reverse, or remove Crowns to correct duplicates or errors, reverse invalid transactions, address cheating or abuse, comply with law, or protect integrity. Adjustments are recorded in wallet and applicable audit records.

## 6. Expiration and Closure

Crowns currently have no automated inactivity expiration. RFL may later introduce prospective expiration after notice where lawful.

If an account or Service closes, unused Crowns may become inaccessible without cash compensation, except where law requires otherwise.
