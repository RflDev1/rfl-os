# PlayRFL Marketplace Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026  
Version: 1.0-draft

The Marketplace lets eligible users transfer Cards for Crowns within RFL.

## 1. Eligibility

You must have an active, profile-completed account and own the Card you list. Suspended or deactivated users may lose access. You may not purchase your own listing.

## 2. Listings

The Service verifies ownership, requires a price within configured limits, and publishes the Card, price, and seller display name. An active unsold listing may be cancelled through supported controls. Cancellation cannot undo a completed sale.

## 3. Purchases

The buyer must have enough Crowns. In one database transaction, RFL rechecks the listing, debits the buyer, credits the seller, transfers the Card, and marks the listing sold. Idempotency and locks reduce duplicate purchases.

The current fee is zero, so proceeds equal the listing price. RFL may add a prospective fee after updating the interface or Policy.

## 4. Card Rights

A purchase transfers only the in-Service Card record. It does not transfer copyright, trademark, Fighter publicity rights, reproduction rights, RFL software or metadata, any physical item, or money. One-of-one and other limited Cards have no guaranteed market or cash value.

## 5. Prohibited Conduct

Do not:

- arrange real-money, cryptocurrency, goods, or service payments;
- sell accounts or promise unsupported access;
- use alternate accounts, collusion, wash trades, fake scarcity, or deceptive claims;
- exploit stale listings, ownership races, duplicates, or errors;
- impersonate sellers or affiliations; or
- use the Marketplace to launder value or evade sanctions.

## 6. Reversals and Disputes

RFL may freeze, cancel, or reverse a transaction affected by unauthorized access, fraud, exploitation, duplication, error, or rule violations. Because Cards can be resold, correction may involve related transactions.

There is no buyer/seller messaging, escrow, offer, refund, chargeback, or automated dispute system. Submit an official Discord ticket promptly with listing and transaction details.

RFL does not guarantee a price, buyer, seller, liquidity, or resale opportunity.

## 7. Supply and Packs

Administrators control supply through card sets, published pack drop tables, rarity weights, and optional maximum supply. Pack openings retain the drop-table version used. Duplicates may occur unless prevented by supply exhaustion.

## 8. Enforcement and Contact

RFL may remove listings, restrict access, adjust Crowns or Cards, suspend accounts, and retain relevant records to enforce these rules.
